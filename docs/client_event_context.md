# Client, RM Note and Event Context

This report combines `data/clients.csv`, `data/rm_notes.json`, and
`data/event_log.csv` into a client-centric view for Priscilla Ong. Events are
linked to a client when the event's transmission channel matches the client's
stated objectives, RM notes, or known outlook. This is contextual analysis, not
an attribution of portfolio performance: holdings and snapshot comparisons are
still required before making quantitative claims.

## Event key

- **Gold cycle:** gold reached successive records in January 2026 and then
  consolidated. Relevant to clients using gold as a hedge or expressing a
  monetary-inflation view.
- **Middle East / Strait shock:** military operations began on 28 February;
  the Strait of Hormuz effectively closed on 4 March; oil peaked around 4 May;
  a renewed blockade on 5 August widened the energy risk premium. Relevant to
  energy, shipping, LNG, Gulf, inflation-sensitive and structured-product
  exposures.
- **Rates shock:** central banks responded to energy inflation on 30 April;
  the Fed delayed easing on 17 June; the US 10-year reached about 4.71% on 29
  July. Relevant to long-duration bonds, rate-sensitive credit, EUR assets and
  USD liquidity.
- **Technology drawdown:** megacap technology briefly lost about USD 2tn on 5
  June before recovering. Relevant to concentrated technology exposure,
  structured notes and collateralised lending.
- **Private-credit stress:** non-traded private-credit vehicles continued to
  experience redemption stress at the 30 June half-year close. Relevant to
  gated funds and clients with near-term cash needs.
